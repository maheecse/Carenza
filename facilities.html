Life-Threatening Emergency?

If experiencing severe chest pain, loss of consciousness, or heavy bleeding, contact hotline services immediately.

Call 999 (National Helpline) Call 112 (Universal)

Immediate Checklist ("What To Do Now")

1

Stay Calm and Assess Safety

Ensure the area is safe from hazards before assisting.

2

Call Emergency Dispatch

State your exact location and summarize patient status clearly.

3

Prepare Essential ID & Records

Gather blood type, allergy info, and contact numbers for paramedics.
