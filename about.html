About CAERNZA

Empowering healthcare navigation through clear design.

Platform Mission

CAERNZA was built to simplify the process of understanding primary care options, locating certified healthcare facilities, and storing medical schedule records.

Safety & Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer

IMPORTANT: CAERNZA provides information and navigation support only. It is not a diagnostic system and does not replace professional clinical evaluation.

Data Encryption & Privacy

All reminders, documents, and scheduling interactions are stored strictly on your device using local browser storage (`localStorage`).
